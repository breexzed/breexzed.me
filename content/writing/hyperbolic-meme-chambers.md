With more and more of human work trending towards reasoning and implementation concerns plummeting there’s only one reason things haven’t exploded: the uneven distribution of human capacity to reason.

For a large amount of our existence we’ve compensated with having “skills”. A finite, rigid sense of reasoning though would be to assume skills no longer have meaning. How can you reason without knowing what to reason with, about some presupposed reason for said reasoning.

Judgement, Taste etc. These are the things that come to mind next when we think beyond the confines of our previous thoughtscape. But how does judgment/taste arise when ideas can be collapsed into reality within moments of their conceptualization.

Judgment, Taste, Intuition, Pattern Recognition within Art. These are things forged in the fire of scarcity, time and physical limitations. It was the grueling process of having to face being judged and deterred that refined the master's work. Now, even I can rewrite x in rust.

The true bottleneck reveals itself naturally: human thought, reasoning, but now: at scale. Because one man's work carries less weight than it used to. The return of the collective hyper-memetic chambers, reality as real-time mirror of human impulse, planetary nervous systems.

Concentration of human collective reasoning like gravitational pulls, actualizing ether into existence at hyperscales, within minuscules of thought. The death of objective curation. Enter the collaborative real-time calculation of attention in hyperbolic meme chambers.

That’s what you get when anyone can generate high-production-value videos, apps, and manifestos in three seconds: the sheer volume of "reality" exploding exponentially. How could the human mind parse infinite streams of instantly collapsed realities without outsourcing.

We see it already in algorithmic feedback loops. The people are tired having realized how wired they were to oppose what’s human and beautiful in return for algorithmic bursts. Enter the collaborative real-time calculation of attention in hyperbolic meme chambers.

hyperbolic meme chambers, human interfaces with the overwhelming sea of instant realities. contextual density, irony, speed, and tribal signaling. If an idea cannot be memed, it cannot be sequenced into the attention economy.

Chambers. they select for what spreads. but spreading isn't judgment. If curation is dead and memeability decides what survives it is the hyperbolic chambers you're in decide what you live with. attention has already gathered there to shift reality, whether you like it or not.

Knowing how to make someone notice things exists in a world where everyone else is also instantly building things is the art, hyperbolic meme chambers are the interfaces, be prepared for high-speed cultural navigation, find hyperbolic meme chambers.

Refuse to live in a world you do not consciously participate in steering.
